Zombs

zombs is a top-down shooter. The world has been taken over by the Undead and they created zombies from all the people they zombified. Other than that, there is literally NO story!! enjoy the mindless violence

Controls: WASD or Arrow Keys to move. Mouse to aim and click to shoot. R to reload. Beware of tripping! You have a 1% chance to trip - halting movement briefly. Don't run forever!
